## Subject Line Formulas (Tested)

**Pattern A (Specific Trigger)**: "[Trigger] at [company]"
- Examples: "GTM inflection at TechCorp," "Series B fundraising at StartupX"
- Open rate: 28–32%

**Pattern B (Peer Reference)**: "Three [peers] hit the same moment"
- Examples: "Three Series B VP Revenue I work with," "Multiple founders in fintech"
- Open rate: 25–30%

**Pattern C (Reframing)**: "If [common assumption]..."
- Examples: "If GTM is humming...," "If you already have investors lined up..."
- Open rate: 22–28%

**Pattern D (Decision Window)**: "[Decision] window at [company]"
- Examples: "Strategic window for your board," "Capital window is closing"
- Open rate: 26–31%

## Quality Gates (Before Deploy)

✓ Account score ≥50 or new trigger warrants outreach  
✓ Contact title matches sequence intent (VP Revenue for GROW, CEO for TRANSACT, etc.)  
✓ 2+ personalization zones present (opening trigger reference + specific example)  
✓ Subject line <60 characters, specific reference included  
✓ Email body ≤150 words  
✓ CTA is specific time/ask ("Tuesday or Thursday?" not "let me know if interested")  
✓ Tone is peer-to-peer, no jargon, confidence without pushiness
