# Testing & Validation Plan: 30-Day Pilot Framework

Strategic approach to validating core assumptions of the growth strategy before full-scale launch. Purpose: Reduce risk, identify quick wins, optimize channel mix, and build confidence in the system.

## Philosophy

**Test Before Scaling**: Don't deploy 75 emails/week if 20 emails/week can't prove the model works.

**Assumption-Driven**: Every element of the strategy is based on assumptions. This plan validates them with data.

**Rapid Learning Loops**: 7-day micro-cycles with clear go/no-go decisions.

**Bias Toward Action**: Start small, iterate fast, scale what works.

## Core Assumptions to Test

### Assumption 1: ICP Targeting Works
**Claim**: Companies $10M–$100M with identified decision triggers will respond to personalized outreach

**Test**: 50 highly targeted cold emails (GROW sequence) to accounts scoring ≥70 on account-scoring model

**Success Metric**: 
- ✅ Open rate ≥22%
- ✅ Reply rate ≥4%
- ✅ 1–2 qualified conversations

**Failure Threshold**: <18% open rate or <2% reply rate

---

### Assumption 2: Messaging Resonates
**Claim**: Four decision-category sequences (GROW/TRANSACT/TRANSFORM/CAPITALIZE) will resonate with different personas

**Test**: 10 emails per sequence (40 total) to relevant contacts, tracking reply quality

**Success Metric**:
- ✅ ≥50% of replies show genuine interest (not "unsubscribe")
- ✅ GROW and TRANSACT sequences outperform TRANSFORM/CAPITALIZE

**Failure Threshold**: <30% genuine interest rate or all sequences performing equally poorly

---

### Assumption 3: LinkedIn Authority Drives Inbound
**Claim**: 2 posts/week + daily engagement will generate 3–5 inbound inquiries/week by week 4

**Test**: Launch LinkedIn program with 2 posts/week (8 posts month 1), daily engagement routine, 50-person relationship strategy

**Success Metric**:
- ✅ Week 1–2: 50–100 profile visits
- ✅ Week 3–4: 2–3 inbound inquiries
- ✅ Posts achieve 3–5% engagement rate

**Failure Threshold**: <2% engagement rate or zero inbound by week 4

---

### Assumption 4: Referral Partners Say Yes
**Claim**: 10–15 Tier 1 partners (M&A attorneys, bankers, PE operators) will commit to referrals in 90 days

**Test**: Conduct 10 partner conversations (week 1–3), pitch partnership model, track commitment rate

**Success Metric**:
- ✅ 6–8 partners commit to referrals
- ✅ 2–3 warm introductions by day 30

**Failure Threshold**: <4 partners committed or 0 introductions by day 30

---

### Assumption 5: Lead-to-Call Conversion
**Claim**: MQL-to-SQL conversion rate will be ≥50% (MQL = 50+ points, SQL = 75+ points)

**Test**: Track 20 MQL through full qualification process

**Success Metric**:
- ✅ 10+ of 20 MQL convert to SQL (50%+)
- ✅ Average sales cycle: 7–14 days from first contact to call booked

**Failure Threshold**: <30% conversion or >21 day sales cycle

- [ ] Document findings

**Days 18–21: Scaled Deployment**
- [ ] Deploy 50–75 more emails (focus on top-performing sequences)
- [ ] Increase LinkedIn frequency to 3 posts/week (double down on winner)
- [ ] Continue daily engagement + referral outreach
- [ ] Book discovery calls with all SQL-qualified leads
- [ ] Publish 3 more LinkedIn posts

**Measurement at Day 21**:
- Email: Cumulative open/reply rate, MQL count
- LinkedIn: Cumulative engagement, inbound inquiries
- Pipeline: Total leads, MQL, SQL, calls scheduled
- Revenue: Any early client signals?

**Key Questions**:
- Which sequence outperformed? (Scale it)
- Which underperformed? (Pause or pivot)
- Is LinkedIn generating inbound? (If yes, increase investment)
- Are calls converting? (Validate Shawn's closing ability)
- Are referral partners engaging? (Continue outreach)

**Decision**: SCALE to 100+ leads/month | OPTIMIZE one channel | PIVOT core messaging

**Go/No-Go Decision Framework**:

### ✅ GO: Full Deployment
**Criteria** (must meet 3 of 4):
- ✅ Email open rate ≥22% OR reply rate ≥4%
- ✅ LinkedIn generated 3+ inbound inquiries
- ✅ Created 3+ SQL or 1+ meeting booked
- ✅ 4+ referral partners committed

**Action**: Scale to 100 leads/month (week 5+)

### 🟡 OPTIMIZE: Conditional Scale
**Criteria** (met 1–2 benchmarks):
- Pivot core messaging or ICP
- Double down on 1–2 high-performing channels
- Scale slower (50 leads/month week 5)
- Plan 30-day optimization cycle

**Common Pivots**:
- If email underperformed but LinkedIn worked: Cut email, go all-in on LinkedIn
- If GROW sequence worked but TRANSACT failed: Focus on GROW, pause TRANSACT
- If no inbound yet: Increase LinkedIn posting to 3x/week, extend test 2 weeks
- If calls converting but leads quality low: Tighten ICP, increase account scoring gate

### ❌ NO-GO: Reset
**Criteria** (met <1 benchmark):
- Core assumptions likely invalid
- ICP or messaging fundamentally misaligned
- Consider pivot or pause

**Actions**:
- Diagnose root cause (messaging? targeting? execution?)
- Run 2-week micro-test on single adjusted variable
- If still fails: Reassess business model

## Risk Mitigation During Pilot

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Email performance tanks | No validation of outbound | Daily monitoring; pause if <18% open rate; test new subject lines immediately |
| LinkedIn engagement flat | No validation of content | Increase posting to 3x/week; test new pillar if <2% engagement |
| Low call quality | Can't validate Shawn's close | Tighten MQL/SQL scoring; prioritize warm leads (referrals, inbound) |
| Referral partners ghost | Partnership model fails | Low priority in pilot; validate only with 3+ committed partners by week 4 |
| Shawn doesn't have time | Execution stalls | Pre-schedule calls; batch content; prioritize 20% highest-impact activities |

## Escalation & Adjustments

**If at any point**:
- Email open rate drops below 18% for 3+ days → Pause, investigate, adjust
- LinkedIn engagement stays <2% after 2 weeks → Increase frequency or change pillar
- No calls booked by day 20 → Tighten MQL scoring or prioritize warm leads
- Referral outreach gets 0 commitments by day 14 → Revisit partner pitch or expand list

**Adjustment Authority**:
- Growth Operator can adjust email frequency, subject lines, posting times
- Growth Operator escalates to Shawn: ICP changes, strategy pivots, channel pauses
- Shawn makes final calls on major pivots (drop a sequence, change messaging angle)
