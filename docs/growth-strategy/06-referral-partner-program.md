# Referral & Strategic Partner Program

Generate 15+ qualified introductions per month through structured partnership network. Target: Tier 1 attorneys, bankers, PE operators; Tier 2 VCs, accountants, wealth managers; Tier 3 CEO peer groups, board directors, family offices.

## Partner Segmentation & Value Proposition

### Tier 1: M&A Professionals (Highest Value)

**Who**: M&A attorneys, investment bankers, PE operating partners

**Mutual Value Proposition**:
- **For Shawn**: Direct referrals to portfolio company CEOs, acquisition targets, founders considering exit
- **For Partner**: Retain and add value to portfolio companies; provide strategic planning to founder clients; improve deal outcomes through pre-process alignment

**Partnership Model**:
- 1–2 warm introductions per month to relevant deal prospects
- Quarterly strategy sessions (30 min calls) discussing market trends
- Annual client appreciation event (CEO roundtable)
- No cash referral fees; referrals grounded in genuine mutual value

**Trigger Situations for Referrals**:
- PE partner advising portfolio CEO on GTM/scaling (→ GROW decision)
- M&A banker working with founder considering exit (→ TRANSACT decision)
- Attorney identifying founder in turnaround situation (→ TRANSFORM decision)
- Banker working with company on Series B capital strategy (→ CAPITALIZE decision)

**20-Minute Partner Conversation Framework**:
1. **Opening** (2 min): "I work with CEOs at $10M–$100M navigating major decisions. Want to share something I'm seeing that might be useful to your clients."
2. **Context** (3 min): "I see four types of decisions driving value: GTM inflection, strategic alternatives, turnaround/restructuring, and capital strategy. A lot of my work aligns with deal processes—either pre-process (helping founder or CFO get clear on optimization) or post-acquisition (integration/restructuring)."
3. **Specific Angle** (5 min): "I imagine some of your [portfolio companies / founder clients / deal pipeline] are navigating one of these moments. When they do, I can help them get strategic clarity fast. Typically 2–3 calls, decision framework in place, $X investment."
4. **Ask** (5 min): "Would it make sense to stay connected? When one of your companies hits [GROW/TRANSACT/TRANSFORM/CAPITALIZE] moment, I could offer perspective. No pressure, but thought it was worth exploring."
5. **Follow-Up** (5 min): Agree on touch cadence (monthly newsletter, quarterly call, introduction criteria)

[Partner name]

**WHO TO INTRODUCE TO SHAWN GUNN**

Shawn advises CEOs and boards at $10M–$100M companies navigating four types of decisions:

### 1. GROW (GTM & Revenue Decisions)
**Who**: CEO, VP Revenue  
**Signal**: Series B completed, GTM plateau, board expects 40%+ YoY growth  
**Typical challenge**: "How do we scale GTM when we've outgrown our current model?"  
**Referral line**: "You'd benefit from Shawn's perspective on GTM architecture at your stage."

### 2. TRANSACT (M&A & Strategic Alternatives)
**Who**: Founder, CEO, Board Chair  
**Signal**: Acquisition interest, founder exit window, strategic buyer approaching  
**Typical challenge**: "How do we maximize value and stay in control?"  
**Referral line**: "Shawn's helped multiple founders navigate exits—he'd give you useful perspective before any buyer conversations."

### 3. TRANSFORM (Turnaround & Restructuring)
**Who**: CEO, New CFO/COO, Board Chair  
**Signal**: Revenue plateau, operational breakdown, leadership transition, post-acquisition integration  
**Typical challenge**: "How do we turn this around without destroying the business?"  
**Referral line**: "Shawn's worked through several turnarounds—might be useful perspective while you're building your 90-day plan."

### 4. CAPITALIZE (Fundraising & Capital Strategy)
**Who**: CFO, CEO, Investor Relations Lead  
**Signal**: Series B/C/D in pipeline, capital strategy unclear, secondary sale consideration  
**Typical challenge**: "How do we optimize our raise and narrative?"  
**Referral line**: "Shawn helps companies like yours optimize their capital strategy before fundraising—might improve your terms."

## Quarterly CEO Roundtable Concept

**Format**: 90-minute virtual event  
**Audience**: 8–10 high-caliber founders, operating partners, investors  
**Frequency**: Quarterly (4x/year)  
**Theme**: Rotates by pillar (Q1: GROW, Q2: TRANSACT, Q3: TRANSFORM, Q4: CAPITALIZE)

**Agenda** (90 minutes):

**0:00–0:05**: Opening remarks (Shawn)  
- Trend observation: "I'm seeing more founders in [PILLAR] question than ever before..."

**0:05–0:30**: Panel discussion (3 operators/founders sharing perspective on theme)  
- Example (GROW): "How I repositioned GTM post-Series B," "Scaling revenue architecture," "Board expectations for scaling"

**0:30–0:55**: Breakout discussion (attendees share challenges, patterns emerge)  
- Attendees discuss their own decisions in small groups

**0:55–1:30**: Open Q&A and networking  
- Shawn answers questions; attendees connect

**1:30–1:35**: Closing + next roundtable announcement

**Logistics**:
- Zoom link distributed 1 week before
- Recording shared with attendees 24 hours after
- Follow-up email with curated resources/reading
- Partner attendees encouraged to bring portfolio companies/clients

**Referral Value**:
- Partners see Shawn's authority; high-visibility event
- Portfolio companies/clients attend for free (partner benefit)
- Generates new inbound leads (attendees who want follow-up)

## Ethical & Legal Guardrails

### No Cash Referral Fees
- Gunn International does NOT pay cash referral fees
- No "finder's fee" structure
- Partnership is mutual-value based, not transactional

### Confidentiality Protection
- Never share deal details with unrelated parties
- Do not discuss one partner's portfolio with another
- If deal is confidential, secure partner agreement before discussing

### Compliance for Regulated Professionals

**M&A Attorneys**:
- Follow ethics rules on conflicts of interest (don't refer if conflict exists)
- Respect attorney-client privilege (don't ask for privileged info)
- Don't circumvent attorney relationship (partner stays primary advisor)

**Accountants (CPA)**:
- Respect audit independence rules (don't refer if audit conflict exists)
- Follow tax secrecy guidelines (don't share tax strategies without permission)
- CPA remains primary advisor on tax/financial strategy

**Financial Advisors/Wealth Managers**:
- Coordinate with existing advisor (don't replace)
- Follow SEC/FINRA guidelines on conflicts
- Don't position as tax/financial advice (positioning is strategic/operational only)

**Investment Bankers**:
- Respect exclusivity agreements (if banker has exclusive mandate, don't interfere)
- Don't position as competing banker
- Coordinate as advisory resource pre-process
