# 90-Day Growth Strategy: Complete Framework

## Executive Summary

**Goal**: Generate 100 identifiable, ICP-relevant leads per month and close at least one new client per month through integrated inbound and outbound channels.

**Execution Model**: One principal (Shawn) + one part-time Growth Operator  
**Investment**: ~$400/month tools + ~$1,200/month Growth Operator  
**Timeline**: 30 days foundation → 60 days scaling → 90 days sustainable operations

## Target Funnel (Monthly, Steady State)

```
2,000 website visitors
    ↓ 5% conversion
100 raw leads
    ↓ 35% qualify
35 MQL (Marketing-Qualified Leads)
    ↓ 50% sales-ready
18 SQL (Sales-Qualified Leads)
    ↓ 60% show-up
10 completed calls
    ↓ 35% opportunity-qualified
3-4 Four-Lens proposals
    ↓ 30%+ close rate
1-2 clients closed
```

**Key Metric**: 2,000 visitors → 1 client closed

## Five-Channel Acquisition Strategy

### Channel 1: Founder-Led LinkedIn (Shawn)
**Monthly Target**: 15–20 inbound inquiries → 8–10 MQL  
**Execution**:
- 2 posts per week (operating insights, decision cases, contrarian takes)
- Daily 20-min engagement routine
- 50-person strategic relationship cultivation
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator for prospect research

**Owner**: Shawn Gunn  
**Time**: 5–6 hours/week

### Channel 2: Account-Based Outbound (Growth Operator)
**Monthly Target**: 40–50 SQL → 8–12 warm leads → 2–3 calls booked  
**Execution**:
- 50–75 personalized cold emails per week
- 5-minute account research template
- Four decision-category sequences
- Weekly trigger monitoring

**Owner**: Growth Operator  
**Time**: 4–5 hours/week

### Channel 3: Referral & Partner Development (Shawn + Growth Op)
**Monthly Target**: 8–12 qualified introductions → 4–6 warm leads → 1–2 calls  
**Execution**:
- 10–12 Tier 1 partnerships developed in 90 days
- Mutual-value positioning for each partner category
- Monthly partner communications
- Quarterly CEO roundtable event

**Owners**: Shawn (relationships) + Growth Op (operations)  
**Time**: 2–3 hours/week combined

### Channel 4: High-Intent Search Content (Shawn + Contractor)
**Monthly Target**: 20–30 organic email signups → 5–8 qualified calls  
**Execution**:
- 2 blog posts per month (800–1200 words each)
- SEO optimization for decision-related keywords
- Email gate with lead magnet (decision frameworks)
- LinkedIn promotion of content

**Owner**: Shawn (direction) + contractor (execution)  
**Time**: 2 hours/week editorial

### Channel 5: Retargeting & Re-engagement (Growth Operator)
**Monthly Target**: 10–15 re-engaged conversations → 1–2 calls  
**Execution**:
- Email nurture series (2-week cadence)
- LinkedIn retargeting ads (if budget allows)
- Win-back campaigns for past MQLs
- Trigger-based timely outreach

**Owner**: Growth Operator  
**Time**: 1–2 hours/week

## ICP Definition (Precise Targeting)

### ICP Segment 1: Growth Decision Leaders
- **Revenue**: $15M–$75M (sweet spot: $25M–$50M)
- **Stage**: Scaling; market validation complete
- **Sectors**: SaaS, healthcare tech, fintech, climate tech, e-commerce platforms
- **Decision Trigger**: GTM plateau, monetization pressure, expansion, board expectations
- **Decision Maker**: CEO/founder (primary), VP Revenue (secondary)
- **Urgency Signal**: Decision in next 30–90 days

### ICP Segment 2: Transaction & Strategic-Alternative Leaders
- **Revenue**: $20M–$100M
- **Status**: Considering acquisition, divestiture, or strategic alternatives
- **Decision Trigger**: Founder exit signal, board pressure, acquisition interest
- **Decision Maker**: CEO/founder, board chair, CFO
- **Urgency Signal**: Process or timeline <6 months

### ICP Segment 3: Turnaround & Restructuring Leaders
- **Revenue**: $10M–$100M
- **Status**: Stagnating or post-acquisition integration crisis
- **Decision Trigger**: Revenue decline, execution breakdown, leadership transition
- **Decision Maker**: CEO, board chair, new CFO/COO
- **Urgency Signal**: Board pressure or crisis

### ICP Segment 4: Capitalization & Investor-Relations Leaders
- **Revenue**: $15M–$100M
- **Status**: Venture-backed, pre-IPO, or recapitalization candidate
- **Decision Trigger**: Fundraising round, secondary sale, investor pressure
- **Decision Maker**: CEO, CFO, board member
- **Urgency Signal**: Fundraising timeline <18 months

## Exclusion Criteria (Do Not Pursue)

**Absolute Excludes**:
- Revenue <$10M or >$100M
- Pre-PMF startups
- Non-profit or government agencies
- Financial distress (Chapter 11, hostile takeover)
- Non-English-speaking primary markets

**Probable Excludes** (re-assess quarterly):
- PE portfolio company with active PE advisor
- Currently using Big 3 consulting firm on same decision
- Founder/CEO with poor reputation
- Company already made decision (hired competitor)

## Lead Definitions & Scoring

### Raw Lead
Any contact who engaged with content or was contacted. No qualification yet.

### Engaged Lead
Raw lead with 2+ engagement signals (email open, reply, LinkedIn engagement, website visit) within 7 days.

### MQL (Marketing-Qualified Lead)
- Company $10M–$100M ✓
- Decision trigger identified ✓
- Urgency signal confirmed ✓
- Decision-maker title verified ✓
- Minimum score: 50 points

### SQL (Sales-Qualified Lead)
- MQL criteria ✓
- Direct conversation initiated ✓
- BANT partially confirmed (budget, authority, need, timing)
- Decision timeline <180 days ✓
- Minimum score: 75 points

### Opportunity
- SQL criteria ✓
- 30+ minute discovery call completed ✓
- Decision clearly articulated ✓
- Four-Lens fit confirmed ✓
- Next step agreed ✓

### Client
- Engagement agreement signed ✓
- Payment received ✓
- Engagement start date confirmed ✓

## Lead Scoring Model (0–100 Points)

### Company Fit (Max 30 points)
- Revenue: $10M–$30M (10pts) | $30M–$100M (20pts) | Outside (0pts)
- Stage: Growth/scaling/transaction-ready (10pts) | Turnaround (8pts) | Early-stage (0pts)
- Sector: SaaS/FinTech/HealthTech/Cleantech (5pts) | Other B2B (3pts) | B2C (0pts)

### Decision Severity & Urgency (Max 35 points)
- Trigger: Major decision (15pts) | Exploratory (8pts) | Unrelated (0pts)
- Timeline: 30–90 days (15pts) | 90–180 days (10pts) | 180+ days (3pts)
- Public signal: Recent funding/M&A/leadership (5pts) | None (0pts)

### Authority & Engagement (Max 20 points)
- Title: CEO/Founder (10pts) | CFO/COO (6pts) | VP-level (2pts)
- Engagement: Replied/booked call (10pts) | Email opened (5pts) | No engagement (0pts)

### Lead Source Quality (Max 15 points)
- Source: Referral (15pts) | Targeted outbound (10pts) | Organic content (8pts) | Broad blast (0pts)

## KPI Dashboard

### Lagging Indicators (Monthly)
- Total clients closed (target: ≥1)
- New contract value (target: $25K–40K)
- Customer acquisition cost (target: $2–3K)
- Sales cycle length (target: 45–60 days)

### Leading Indicators (Weekly)
- Raw leads (target: 25/week)
- MQL rate (target: 35%)
- MQL→SQL rate (target: 50%)
- Email open rate (target: 25–35%)
- Email reply rate (target: 5–8%)
- LinkedIn engagement (target: 3–5%)
- Profile visits (target: 50–100/month)
- Calls booked (target: 2–4/week)

## Stop/Continue/Scale Rules

### EXPAND (>120% of target)
- Double down on high-performing channel
- Increase frequency or budget allocation
- Test new angles within successful channel

### CONTINUE (80–120% of target)
- Maintain current spend and cadence
- Monitor leading indicators weekly
- Adjust if trends down for 2+ weeks

### STOP (<80% for 2+ weeks)
- Pause that channel
- Investigate root cause
- Reallocate to stronger channels

## Major Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Slow lead gen (months 1–2) | Pipeline thin | Front-load referral; expect exponential growth |
| High MQL but low SQL conversion | Qualification drift | Tighten scoring; review ICP definition weekly |
| Shawn bottleneck | Conversion stuck | Hire fractional operating partner; document decision process |
| Email performance tanks | Channel collapses | Monitor open rate daily; A/B test subjects weekly |
| LinkedIn engagement flat | Low CAC channel fails | Adjust content angle; increase engagement time |

✅ **Month 3**: 100 leads/month, 3–4 calls/week, 1–2 clients closed
