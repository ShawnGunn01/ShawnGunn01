# ABM Operating System: Outbound Mechanics

## Overview

Account-Based Marketing (ABM) execution focused on 50–75 personalized cold emails per week targeting ICP-relevant companies facing one of four major decisions: GROW, TRANSACT, TRANSFORM, or CAPITALIZE. This system is designed to generate 35+ qualified leads per month while protecting Shawn's premium positioning and reputation.

## Target Account Selection

### Step 1: Define Your Target Account List (TAL)

**Selection Criteria**:
- Revenue: $10M–$100M
- Stage: Scaling, transaction-ready, turnaround, or venture-backed
- Sector: SaaS, FinTech, HealthTech, Cleantech, e-commerce platforms
- Recent signal: Funding within 12 months, leadership change, acquisition news, or M&A activity
- CEO/founder publicly active (LinkedIn, Crunchbase, press mentions)

**Size**: 200–300 target accounts for sustainable 50–75 emails/week

**Build Sources**:
- Apollo.io search (revenue + sector + recent funding/activity filters)
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator (advanced search + saved leads)
- Crunchbase (funding filters, press releases)
- ZoomInfo (optional, requires subscription)

### Step 2: Account Scoring (0–100 Model)

Score each account to prioritize sequence timing and resource allocation.

**Company Fundamentals (Max 30 points)**:
- Revenue size: $10M–$30M (10pts) | $30M–$100M (20pts)
- Growth rate: Funded/acquisition interest (10pts) | Bootstrapped (5pts)
- Sector fit: SaaS/FinTech/HealthTech (5pts) | Other B2B (3pts)

**Decision Trigger & Urgency (Max 40 points)**:
- Trigger strength: Major decision imminent (20pts) | Exploratory (10pts) | Unrelated (0pts)
- Public signal: Recent funding/M&A/leadership change (15pts) | None (3pts)
- Timeline: 30–90 days out (5pts) | 90+ days (2pts)

**Founder/CEO Fit (Max 20 points)**:
- CEO profile: Public speaker/thought leader/board active (10pts) | Private (3pts)
- Prior exits: Yes (5pts) | No (0pts)
- Social proof: Strong network visible on LinkedIn (5pts) | Private (0pts)

**Source Quality (Max 10 points)**:
- Referral/warm introduction possible (10pts)
- Organic inbound or Shawn's prior network (5pts)
- Cold outbound (3pts)

**Score Interpretation**:
- 80–100: Priority 1 (email immediately, sequence #1)
- 60–79: Priority 2 (sequence in weeks 2–3)
- 40–59: Priority 3 (sequence in weeks 4–5)
- <40: Hold or exclude

## Contact Research & Selection

### Contact Hierarchy by Decision Category

For each target account, research and identify up to 3 decision-makers in order of outreach priority.

**GROW Sequence (GTM/revenue decisions)**:
1. VP Revenue / Chief Revenue Officer (primary)
2. CEO / Founder (secondary)
3. VP Product (tertiary)

**TRANSACT Sequence (M&A/strategic decisions)**:
1. CEO / Founder (primary)
2. Board Chair / Lead Director (secondary)
3. CFO (tertiary)

**TRANSFORM Sequence (turnaround/restructuring)**:
1. CEO / Founder (primary)
2. New CFO / COO (secondary)
3. Board Chair (tertiary)

**CAPITALIZE Sequence (fundraising/investor relations)**:
1. CFO / Investor Relations Lead (primary)
2. CEO / Founder (secondary)
3. Board Member / Investor (tertiary)

### 5-Minute Company Research Template

Before sending email #1 to an account:

1. **Firmographic** (2 min):
   - Latest funding round (amount, date, investors)
   - Recent leadership changes (hires, exits, new board)
   - Recent press/news (M&A rumors, expansion, crisis)

2. **Trigger Signal** (1 min):
   - Identify the specific trigger (e.g., "Series B raised $20M in Apr 2024, new VP Revenue hired, GTM at plateau")
   - Map to decision category (GROW/TRANSACT/TRANSFORM/CAPITALIZE)

3. **Decision Maker Profile** (1 min):
   - LinkedIn title confirmation
   - Prior experience (exits, board roles)
   - Public speaking/thought leadership (podcasts, articles, board memberships)

4. **Personalization Hook** (1 min):
   - Find 1–2 specific, credible angles to reference in email
   - Examples: "Saw you just raised Series B," "Three CEOs from [sector] I've worked with faced similar scaling inflection," "Your COO's background in [industry]"

## Trigger Monitoring System

### Weekly Monitoring Routine (1 hour/week)

**Monday Morning**:
1. Monitor TAL for news: Set Google Alerts for each company; check Crunchbase daily digest
2. Identify new triggers: Funding, leadership, M&A rumors, earnings miss, expansion announcements
3. Create micro-list: Pull 5–10 accounts that triggered this week; create research notes

**Categorize Trigger Type**:
- **Funding trigger**: Recent capital raise (Series A/B/C, debt, venture debt)
  - Message: Position Shawn as advisor to high-growth GTM transitions
- **Leadership trigger**: New CEO, CFO, COO, VP Revenue, board member
  - Message: New leader needs external perspective on key decisions
- **M&A trigger**: Acquisition news, founder exit signal (not in current company), PE interest
  - Message: Portfolio integration, post-acquisition optimization
- **Operational trigger**: Public commentary on growth plateau, revenue headwinds, restructuring
  - Message: Specific insight on the decision they're likely facing

## Four Email Sequences (Decision-Category Based)

### GROW Sequence (Series B GTM Inflection)
**Target**: VP Revenue, CEOs at growth plateau  
**Context**: Company has product-market fit; now scaling GTM  
**Length**: 5 emails over 14 days

**Email 1 — Problem Recognition**
- Subject: "GTM inflection point at [company]"
- Hook: Specific Series B signal or revenue growth stage
- CTA: "30-minute call to compare playbooks?"

**Email 2 — Social Proof (3 days later)**
- Subject: "Three others in [sector] hit the same moment"
- Body: Brief case study of comparable company
- Personalization: Add specific detail (investor, stage, timing)
- CTA: "Thoughts on approach?"

**Email 3 — Objection Handle (6 days later)**
- Subject: "If GTM is humming..."
- Address likely objection: "We're already executing well"
- Reframe: "The inflection point isn't about tactics—it's about structural decisions"
- CTA: "Worth 20 minutes?"

**Email 4 — Urgency (10 days later)**
- Subject: "Timing on this"
- Frame: "Most executives in your position decide this Q"
- CTA: "Final availability this month?"

**Email 5 — Close (14 days later)**
- Subject: "One final thought"
- Brief, high-level observation on market/timing
- CTA: "Let me know if/when you're ready to explore"

### TRANSACT Sequence (M&A & Strategic Alternatives)
**Target**: CEO, Board Chair, CFO  
**Context**: Company considering sale, acquisition, or strategic alternative  
**Length**: 5 emails over 14 days

**Email 1 — Trigger Recognition**
- Subject: "Strategic moment for [company]"
- Hook: Funding signal, M&A rumors, founder exit window
- CTA: "Brief strategic call?"

**Email 2 — Social Proof**
- Subject: "Helped 4 founders navigate this exact decision"
- Body: Transaction outcome examples (values, timing, outcomes)
- CTA: "Worth comparing?"

**Email 3 — Objection**
- Subject: "If you're 'just exploring'"
- Reframe: Decision-process clarity = better outcomes
- CTA: "Value in that conversation?"

**Email 4 — Urgency**
- Subject: "M&A windows close fast"
- Frame: "Buyers are active now; windows shift Q-to-Q"
- CTA: "Make sense to align?"

**Email 5 — Close**
- Subject: "Last check-in on this"
- CTA: "I'll assume you're not pursuing now; happy to reconnect if that changes"

### TRANSFORM Sequence (Turnaround & Restructuring)
**Target**: CEO, New CFO/COO, Board Chair  
**Context**: Company stagnating, post-acquisition crisis, leadership transition  
**Length**: 5 emails over 14 days

**Email 1 — Problem**
- Subject: "On the restructuring at [company]"
- Hook: Press on decline, leadership change, board pressure
- CTA: "Perspective on next 90 days?"

**Email 2 — Social Proof**
- Subject: "Turned around 3 in [sector]"
- Case study: Revenue recovery, margin improvement, or team restructure
- CTA: "Talk through your situation?"

**Email 3 — Objection**
- Subject: "If you think it's just a sales/ops problem"
- Reframe: Turnarounds are multi-dimensional; Shawn's "four-lens" framework
- CTA: "30 min to map it?"

**Email 4 — Urgency**
- Subject: "6-month decision window"
- Frame: Board/investors won't wait; decisions must be clear by month 3
- CTA: "Urgent window on this—available this week?"

**Email 5 — Close**
- Subject: "One final perspective"
- CTA: "Let me know if/when you want to explore"

### CAPITALIZE Sequence (Fundraising & Investor Relations)
**Target**: CFO, Investor Relations Lead, CEO  
**Context**: Company in fundraising or pursuing secondary/recapitalization  
**Length**: 5 emails over 14 days

**Email 1 — Trigger**
- Subject: "On [company]'s capital strategy"
- Hook: Fundraising timeline, recent institutional investor addition, secondary sale
- CTA: "Optimize your raise strategy?"

**Email 2 — Social Proof**
- Subject: "Helped 5 Series B's add $5M–$40M"
- Case study: Capital strategy, timing, investor positioning
- CTA: "Compare notes?"

**Email 3 — Objection**
- Subject: "If you have investors lined up"
- Reframe: Strategy ≠ execution; pre-raise clarity improves terms
- CTA: "Worth a quick call?"

**Email 4 — Urgency**
- Subject: "Capital windows have short shelves"
- Frame: "Post-earnings, post-product drop-offs, or recession triggers—investor appetite shifts fast"
- CTA: "Let's align now?"

**Email 5 — Close**
- Subject: "Last note on this"
- CTA: "Circle back when you're ready"

## Role-Specific Messaging Variations

### CEO / Founder Angle
- Emphasize: Board expectations, founder legacy, exit optionality, decision framework
- Tone: Peer-to-peer, strategic, high-confidence
- Subject lines: "Founder moment," "Strategic decision," "Legacy question"

### Board Chair Angle
- Emphasize: Governance, fiduciary duty, precedent across portfolio, risk mitigation
- Tone: Formal, trustworthy, precedent-based
- Subject lines: "Board perspective," "Governance angle," "Portfolio view"

### CFO Angle
- Emphasize: Financial modeling, capital efficiency, investor narrative, risk quantification
- Tone: Data-driven, analytical, outcome-focused
- Subject lines: "Capital strategy," "Financial model," "Investor narrative"

### Chief of Staff / COO Angle
- Emphasize: Operational execution, scaling systems, team structure, decision architecture
- Tone: Execution-focused, pragmatic, implementation-ready
- Subject lines: "Execution question," "Scaling inflection," "Operational decision"

## Subject Line Formula

**Pattern**: [Trigger or role insight] + [Implicit offer] + [Personalization]

**Examples**:
- "GTM inflection at [company]" (GROW)
- "Strategic moment for [founder]" (TRANSACT)
- "On the turnaround" (TRANSFORM)
- "Capital strategy for [company]" (CAPITALIZE)

**Quality Checklist**:
- ✓ Specific reference (company, person, public signal)
- ✓ Assumes industry knowledge (not overly educational)
- ✓ Implies value without being pushy
- ✓ <60 characters
- ✓ No "?" if avoidable (appears tentative)

## Personalization Zones (Required in Each Email)

**Zone 1: Opening (first sentence)**
- Specific trigger or public signal: "[Name], saw you raised Series B in April" OR "Your expansion into [market] is a classic inflection point"

**Zone 2: Social Proof**
- 1 specific, credible reference: "Three Series B heads of revenue I've worked with faced this exact moment" OR "Helped [CEO name] through a similar transition post-acquisition"

**Zone 3: Point of View**
- One declarative, confident statement: "The bottleneck at [stage] is rarely tactics—it's architecture" OR "Most CEOs underestimate how much of a turnaround is structural vs. tactical"

**Zone 4: CTA**
- Specific, time-bound ask: "30 min this week?" OR "Available Tuesday or Thursday for 20 minutes?"

## Follow-Up Strategy

### Email Cadence
- Email 1 → Email 2 (3 days)
- Email 2 → Email 3 (3 days)
- Email 3 → Email 4 (4 days)
- Email 4 → Email 5 (4 days)
- Total sequence: 14 days

**If reply received on Email 1–3**:
- Skip to personalized follow-up call/message within 24 hours
- Do NOT send remaining templated emails

**If no reply by Email 5**:
- Move account to nurture queue (once-monthly touch)
- Re-engage if new trigger occurs

### LinkedIn Follow-Up (Optional, High-Touch)

After Email 3 (if no reply):
- Send LinkedIn connection request + brief DM: "Saw my email; wanted to loop in here too. Happy to jump on a call if it's relevant."
- Do not send if already connected

## A/B Testing Framework

### Test Variables (Rotate weekly)

**Subject Line Tests**:
- Test A: Trigger-specific ("GTM inflection at X")
- Test B: Role-based ("For the VP Revenue at X")
- Measure: Open rate (target: 25–35%)

**CTA Tests**:
- Test A: Direct offer ("30 min call?")
- Test B: Soft offer ("Worth an exploratory chat?")
- Measure: Reply rate (target: 5–8%)

**Personalization Tests**:
- Test A: Specific company trigger ("Series B in April")
- Test B: Sector peer reference ("Three Series B heads I worked with")
- Measure: Reply quality (yes/no/maybe ratio)

### Results Target
- Aim for 3-email rotation; once 1 performs >30% open rate, adopt as default
- Test 50 emails with variation A, 50 with variation B each month
- Pause underperformers; scale winners

## Weekly Activity Targets

**Volume**: 50–75 personalized emails/week
- Monday–Friday: 10–15 emails/day
- Each email: 5-minute research + 3-minute personalization

**Quality Gates** (quality > volume):
- Every email must include 2+ personalization zones
- No templated, generic subject lines
- Every email references 1+ specific trigger or public signal
- Reply rate target: 5–8% (8–15 replies/week from 200–250 emails)

## Quality Control Checklist

Before deploying each email:
- ✓ Account revenue in $10M–$100M range
- ✓ Account score ≥40 (or new trigger warrants outreach)
- ✓ Contact title matches sequence (CEO for TRANSACT, VP Revenue for GROW, etc.)
- ✓ Trigger identified and documented in CRM
- ✓ 2+ personalization zones present (opening + specific example + POV)
- ✓ Subject line <60 chars, specific, no generic qualifier
- ✓ Email body ≤150 words
- ✓ CTA is specific (not "let me know if interested")
- ✓ Follow-up date set in CRM

## Apollo.io Deployment Guide

### Setup Steps

1. **List Building**:
   - Filter: Revenue $10M–$100M, sector, recent funding/activity
   - Export 200–300 companies to Apollo
   - Enrich contacts (Apollo auto-pulls CEO, VP Revenue, CFO data)

2. **Sequence Configuration**:
   - Create 4 sequences (GROW, TRANSACT, TRANSFORM, CAPITALIZE)
   - Set email timing: Day 0, +3, +6, +10, +14
   - Enable Apollo tracking (opens, replies)

3. **Email Warm-Up**:
   - Configure domain verification (SPF/DKIM)
   - Enable Apollo's warm-up protocol (starts low volume, ramps to full cadence)
   - Monitor sender reputation score (target: >80)

4. **Lead Routing**:
   - Configure HubSpot integration (auto-sync replies to CRM)
   - Map Apollo fields to HubSpot custom fields
   - Set auto-escalation rule (reply within 1 hour → notify Shawn)

### Monitoring
- Weekly open/reply/bounce rates
- Adjust subject lines if open rate <20%
- Pause domain if bounce rate >5%
- Monitor sender reputation daily
