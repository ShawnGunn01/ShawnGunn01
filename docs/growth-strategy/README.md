# Gunn International Growth Strategy Documentation

Complete operational framework for generating 100+ ICP-relevant leads per month and closing at least one new client per month.

## Overview

This documentation contains the complete growth and business development system for Gunn International, designed to be executed by Shawn Gunn (principal) and one part-time Growth Operator.

**Target**: 100 identifiable, ICP-relevant leads per month → 35 MQL → 18 SQL → 10 completed calls → 3–4 proposals → 1–2 closed clients

**Execution**: Fully scalable across three core channels:
1. **LinkedIn Authority** (25 leads/month) — founder-led content + engagement
2. **Account-Based Outbound** (35 leads/month) — targeted email sequences
3. **Referral & Partner Network** (15+ leads/month) — strategic relationships

## Risks & Mitigation

**Risk**: Slow lead generation in months 1–2
- **Mitigation**: Front-load referral activation; expect exponential growth month 2–3

**Risk**: Email performance below targets
- **Mitigation**: Weekly QC; A/B test subject lines; monitor sender reputation

**Risk**: Shawn becomes a bottleneck
- **Mitigation**: Hire fractional operating partner by month 3; train on proposal process

**Risk**: LinkedIn engagement flat
- **Mitigation**: Adjust content angle; increase posting cadence; improve engagement routine
