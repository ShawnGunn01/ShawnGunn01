# Growth Operator SOP: Standard Operating Procedure

Complete operating manual for the part-time Growth Operator role. Target audience: Growth Operator hire or Shawn executing solo.

## Role Definition

**Title**: Growth Operator (Part-Time)  
**Reporting**: Shawn Gunn  
**Time**: 12 hours/week (flexible; can be 3–4 hour blocks)  
**Compensation**: $1,200–1,600/month (estimated)  
**Success**: Generate 35–40 ICP-relevant leads/month through ABM outbound + LinkedIn logistics + CRM management  

### Success Criteria (Ramp over 3 months)

**Month 1** (Ramping):
- Deploy 150–200 emails/month
- Maintain 22–28% email open rate
- Generate 12–18 raw leads
- 5–8 MQL qualified
- Build foundational systems and processes

**Month 2** (Building):
- Deploy 200–250 emails/month
- Maintain 25–35% email open rate
- Generate 25–30 raw leads
- 8–12 MQL qualified
- Establish consistent cadence

**Month 3+ (Sustainable)**:
- Deploy 250+ emails/month (50–75/week)
- Maintain 25–35% email open rate
- Generate 35–40 raw leads
- 12–15 MQL qualified
- Predictable pipeline generation

## Core Processes

### Process 1: Account Research & Trigger Monitoring (5-Minute Template)

**Use this before deploying every email**

**Step 1: Firmographic Verification (90 seconds)**
- [ ] Company revenue: Confirm $10M–$100M range
  - Source: Apollo, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, company website
- [ ] Latest fundraising: If Series B+, confirm amount + date
- [ ] Recent news: Any press, earnings, M&A rumors?

**Step 2: Trigger Identification (2 minutes)**
- [ ] Trigger type: Funding | Leadership | M&A | Operational
- [ ] Trigger signal: Specific event (e.g., "Series B raised $20M in Apr 2024")
- [ ] Decision category match: GROW | TRANSACT | TRANSFORM | CAPITALIZE
- [ ] Confidence level: High | Medium | Low

**Step 3: Contact Research (90 seconds)**
- [ ] Contact title: CEO | VP Revenue | CFO | Board Chair (confirm match to sequence)
- [ ] Contact credibility: Is this person a decision-maker? (check LinkedIn)
- [ ] Public background: Prior exits, board roles, speaking (confirms seniority)

**Step 4: Personalization Angle (90 seconds)**
- [ ] Identify 2+ specific references for email opening:
  - Specific company trigger ("Series B in Apr")
  - Peer reference ("Three VP Revenue I work with at [sector]")
  - Market observation ("I'm seeing [pattern] in [market]")

**Template Shortcut** (if short on time):
- Company: [Name], Revenue: [Range], Trigger: [Specific event], Contact: [Name/Title], Angle: [Specific reference]

**Example**:
- Company: TechCorp, Revenue: $28M, Trigger: Series B $15M in Mar 2024, Contact: Sarah (VP Revenue), Angle: "Series B in March—GTM inflection moment"

---

### Process 2: Outbound Email Execution

**Daily Workflow (per sequence)**

1. **Pull Account List** (5 min):
   - From Apollo, export today's batch (15–20 accounts for GROW sequence)
   - Filter: Account score ≥50, no prior contact, decision timeline <6 months

2. **Research Each Account** (5 min per account):
   - Use 5-minute research template (see above)
   - Document findings in HubSpot before personalizing

3. **Personalize Email** (3 min per email):
   - Open email template (e.g., GROW Email 1)
   - Replace [first name], [company], trigger reference
   - Personalize subject line per formula
   - Adjust if contact is CEO vs. VP Revenue
   - Proofread (grammar, tone, length <150 words)

4. **Deploy** (1 min per email):
   - Paste into Apollo
   - Set send time (11am local time zone)
   - Confirm recipient email is valid (Apollo auto-checks)
   - Deploy

5. **Log** (1 min per email):
   - Update HubSpot: Create contact record, set "outreach_status" = "Sequence Active"
   - Log send in Google Sheets (date, company, sequence, subject line, send time)

**Total per email**: 14 minutes  
**Per day**: 30–40 emails × 14 min = 7–9 hours equivalent; compressed into 3 hours via batching

### Process 4: Lead Tracking in HubSpot

**Daily Tasks** (ongoing, 20–30 min/day)

1. **Check for Replies**:
   - Apollo auto-syncs email replies to HubSpot
   - Review new contacts added to "Replied" stage
   - Score each reply:
     - **Hot** (genuine interest, ask for call): Escalate to Shawn <1 hour
     - **Warm** (interested, exploratory): Log in HubSpot, wait for Shawn follow-up
     - **Cold** (not a fit, generic reply): Log, move to "Not Responsive"

2. **Update Pipeline**:
   - Move contacts through stages as conversations progress
   - Stages: Raw Contact → Sequence Active → Replied → Meeting Booked → Discovery Call → Proposal → Closed Won

3. **Flag Escalations**:
   - Any "Hot" reply should have a Slack/email notification to Shawn <1 hour
   - Template: "[Prospect name] at [Company] replied to [sequence]. [Brief reply]. Escalating for response."

---

### Process 5: Quality Standards & QC Gates

**Before deploying any email, verify**:

- ✓ Account revenue confirmed in $10M–$100M range
- ✓ Account score ≥40 (or new trigger warrants outreach)
- ✓ Contact title matches sequence (CEO for TRANSACT, VP Revenue for GROW, etc.)
- ✓ Trigger identified and documented in HubSpot
- ✓ 2+ personalization zones present:
  - Opening: Specific trigger reference
  - Example: Peer reference or case study
  - POV: Decision framework or observation
- ✓ Subject line <60 characters, specific (not generic)
- ✓ Email body ≤150 words
- ✓ Email body uses 1st person (casual, not corporate)
- ✓ CTA is specific time/ask ("Tuesday or Thursday?" not "let me know if interested")
- ✓ No jargon, no corporate speak
- ✓ Grammar + tone pass
- ✓ Follow-up date set in CRM

**If QC fails on any box**: Do not deploy. Revise or escalate to Shawn.

### Process 6: Escalation Framework

**Warm Lead (<1 hour SLA)**:
Any email reply showing genuine interest = immediate notification to Shawn

**Escalation Method**:
1. Flag in HubSpot (tag: "escalate-shawn")
2. Send Slack/email to Shawn with 3 lines:
   - Prospect name + company
   - Their reply (exact text)
   - Your recommendation (call? follow-up?)

**Example**:
"Jessica Chen @ RapidScale replied: 'Would be interested in a call about GTM strategy. We're in the middle of a Series B right now.' Recommend: Call this week?"

## Troubleshooting Guide

### Email Performance Drops Below Target

**Symptom**: Open rate drops <22%, reply rate <4%

**Root causes**:
1. Sender reputation issue (domain reputation score)
2. Subject line fatigue (same formula for too long)
3. Send timing mismatch (sending when recipients aren't active)
4. List quality decline (sending to lower-intent accounts)

**Solutions**:
- Check Apollo sender reputation score (should be >80)
- Rotate subject line formula (switch from Pattern A to Pattern C)
- Test new send times (try 9am, 2pm instead of 11am)
- Audit last 20 accounts researched (are they truly ICP?)
- Reduce daily volume 48 hours while investigating
- Report to Shawn if persists >3 days

---

### Low Reply Rate Despite High Open Rate

**Symptom**: 30% open rate but <3% reply rate

**Root causes**:
1. CTA is too vague ("let me know if interested")
2. Email body is too long (>150 words; people don't engage)
3. Personalization feels generic (subject line references real trigger, body doesn't land)
4. Tone is corporate/consultantey (not peer-to-peer)

**Solutions**:
- Review last 10 replies vs. last 10 non-replies
- Tighten CTA: "Tuesday or Thursday for 20 min?" vs. "Interested in a call?"
- Cut email length: Aim for 120 words max
- Audit tone: Does it sound like Shawn's voice or a B2B marketing agency?
- Test "soft offer" CTA variant (Template C in subject lines)
- Report findings to Shawn; adjust template

---

### Flat LinkedIn Engagement (Content Not Resonating)

**Symptom**: <2% engagement rate on posts; <50 profile visits/month

**Root causes**:
1. Content pillar mismatch (posting GROW content to non-growth audience)
2. Post length (if >8 sentences, engagement drops)
3. No call-to-action (posts asking no question = 50% fewer comments)
4. Timing (posting outside peak hours)
5. Posting cadence dropped (frequency < 2x/week)

**Solutions**:
- Review last 4 posts: Which pillar performed best? Shift to that
- Audit post length: Cut to <6 sentences if >8
- Add explicit ask: "What [pillar decision] are you navigating?" at end
- Test send times: Try 8am, 11am, 2pm; track which gets most engagement
- Increase posting frequency to 2x/week minimum
- Report to Shawn with findings; adjust content calendar

---

### Too Many "Not the Right Fit" Replies

**Symptom**: High reply rate but 30%+ say "not relevant" or "wrong person"

**Root causes**:
1. ICP targeting drift (selling to $5M companies instead of $10M+)
2. Sequence misalignment (sending GROW email to CEO in stable company)
3. Personalization miss (mentioning wrong trigger/context)
4. Contact research weak (emailing wrong person for decision)

**Solutions**:
- Audit last 10 "not a fit" accounts:
  - Are they truly $10M–$100M?
  - Did they have a genuine trigger?
  - Did we identify the right decision-maker?
- Tighten account scoring (only deploy if score ≥50)
- Increase research quality: Spend 7 minutes per account, not 5
- Review contact hierarchy: Are we reaching CEO for TRANSACT, VP Revenue for GROW?
- Report findings to Shawn; discuss ICP or targeting adjustment

---

### Overwhelm / Volume Can't Keep Up

**Symptom**: Growth Operator falling behind on research, personalization, or logging

**Root causes**:
1. Weekly targets unsustainable (pushing 75 emails/week too early)
2. Research template too detailed (5-min research taking 15 min)
3. No batching/templates (every email customized from scratch)
4. Onboarding incomplete (unfamiliar with tools, processes)

**Solutions**:
- **Scale down**: Drop to 40–50 emails/week for 2 weeks to catch up
- **Streamline research**: Use template shortcut (company, revenue, trigger, angle only)
- **Template everything**: Use email templates 80%; only customize 3 zones
- **Batch work**: Monday research all week's accounts; don't mix research and deployment
- **Review schedule**: Is 12 hours/week realistic? Discuss with Shawn
- **Get support**: Shawn may help with research Monday or Thursday
