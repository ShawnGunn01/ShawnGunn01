## Day 30 Go/No-Go Decision Framework

### Metrics Review (Thursday, Day 29)

**MUST-HAVES (all 3 must be true)**:
1. Raw leads generated: ≥15 (target: 20–25)
2. Email open rate: ≥22% (target: 25–35%)
3. Daily engagement routine: 5/5 days completed

**GO Decision** (proceed to Month 2 scaling):
- All 3 must-haves met ✓
- At least 1 sequence (GROW/TRANSACT/TRANSFORM/CAPITALIZE) performing above target
- 1+ meetings booked
- Shawn and Growth Operator aligned on next month focus

**PIVOT Decision** (adjust strategy before scaling):
- 1+ must-haves missed
- All sequences performing below target (<18% open rate)
- <1 meeting booked
- Clear data indicating messaging or targeting misalignment

**NO-GO Decision** (pause and reassess):
- 2+ must-haves missed for 2+ weeks
- Fundamental ICP or positioning misalignment evident
- System/tool failures preventing execution

### Pivot Options (if needed)

**Pivot #1: Content Angle**
- Shift from "advisory expertise" to "operator experience"
- Test new content pillars (less thought-leadership, more case studies)
- Increase "Contrarian Note" frequency (higher engagement)

**Pivot #2: Account Targeting**
- Tighten ICP (focus only on $25M–$50M companies)
- Shift to higher-intent triggers (M&A news, funding only)
- Increase CEO/founder focus (vs. VP-level)

**Pivot #3: Email Strategy**
- Reduce email volume; increase personalization depth
- Shift subject lines to "soft offer" variants (Pivot #2 in subject lines)
- Test 3-email sequences instead of 5 (reduce volume, increase relevance)

**Pivot #4: Channel Balance**
- Reduce email focus; increase LinkedIn focus (higher engagement baseline)
- Front-load referral activation (vs. waiting until Month 2)
- Add webinar/content magnet to email sequences
